Three best practices for exception handling are making sure to include the cause of the exception in the stack trace. When wrapping exceptions together it is important to include/pass the original exception in the constructor of a new one. It is always important to know the root cause. Another best practice is to use meaningfull messages in an exception, alwasys use precise and accurate information in an exception message. An unclear message can lead to misunderstandings in how the error acutally occured. Finally, always be specifc in what you catch, do not try and catch and entire method as that dosent show a root cause. Instead try and catch specfic lines within a method to see the root cause of an error.
